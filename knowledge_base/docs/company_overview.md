# Company overview

PranavX Labs is an AI automation studio that designs and builds practical,
production-ready AI systems for businesses. We specialize in turning
repetitive operational workflows — customer support, data entry, lead
qualification, internal reporting — into reliable, automated pipelines
powered by large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, and
workflow orchestration tools.

## What we do

We are not a generic chatbot vendor. Every system we build is designed
around measurable outcomes: faster response times, fewer missed tickets,
consistent answers, and human oversight where it actually matters. Our
philosophy is "automate the repetitive 80%, keep humans in the loop for
the important 20%."

## Founded

PranavX Labs was founded in 2023 by a small team of AI engineers and
automation specialists based in India, with clients across India,
Southeast Asia, and the Middle East.

## Who we work with

We work with small and mid-sized businesses — SaaS companies, e-commerce
brands, and service businesses — that receive a high volume of repetitive
customer queries and want to reduce response times without sacrificing
quality or losing the human touch on complex issues.
