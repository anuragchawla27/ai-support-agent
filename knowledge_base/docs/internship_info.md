# Internship program

## Overview

PranavX Labs runs a 15-day AI Automation Internship for students and
early-career engineers. Interns build one complete, real-world AI
automation project end-to-end — not a toy exercise — covering
architecture design, RAG implementation, intent/ticket classification,
confidence evaluation, human escalation workflows, dashboards, testing,
and deployment.

## Who it's for

Students or recent graduates with basic programming knowledge (any
language) who want hands-on experience with LLMs, retrieval-augmented
generation, and workflow automation tools like n8n. No prior AI/ML
experience is required — the program is structured to teach applied AI
engineering through building.

## Duration and format

15 working days, remote, self-paced within a structured daily roadmap
(architecture → knowledge base → RAG → classification → response
generation → confidence/escalation → dashboard → testing → deployment).
Interns receive milestone check-ins rather than constant supervision.

## Application status

Applicants can check their application status by providing their
registered email or application ID through the support form. Typical
review time is 3-5 business days after submission.

## Certificate and outcomes

Interns who complete the full 15-day roadmap and submit a working demo
receive a completion certificate and a project write-up suitable for
their portfolio or resume.

## How to apply

Applications are accepted on a rolling basis through the PranavX Labs
careers page. No application fee is charged at any stage.
