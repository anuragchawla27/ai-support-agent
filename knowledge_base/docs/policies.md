# Policies

## Refund policy

Refunds for paid engagements may be requested within 7 days of the
initial payment if project work has not yet started. Once development
work has begun, refunds are evaluated case by case and require human
approval — refund decisions are never made automatically by this support
system.

## Data privacy

PranavX Labs does not share client data, source code, or knowledge base
content with third parties. Any data provided for a project (documents,
credentials, sample data) is used solely for that project and deleted
upon request after project completion.

## Support response policy

- General enquiries: response within 1 business day
- Technical issues (active projects): response within 4 business hours
- Payment or billing issues: treated as high priority, escalated to a
  human team member immediately rather than handled automatically

## Escalation policy

Any query involving payments, refunds, legal/compliance matters, or a
customer expressing strong dissatisfaction is automatically escalated to
a human team member. The AI support system does not have authority to
resolve these categories independently, regardless of how confident its
proposed answer is.

## Internship program policy

Internship participation does not guarantee employment. Certificates are
issued only upon successful completion of the full project roadmap and
submission of a working demonstration.
