# Services

PranavX Labs offers the following services:

## 1. AI customer support automation

End-to-end support ticket automation: intent detection, knowledge-base
retrieval (RAG), grounded response generation, confidence scoring, and
human escalation for complex or sensitive cases. Includes a ticket
management dashboard.

## 2. Workflow automation consulting

We audit a business's existing manual workflows (support, operations,
data entry) and design automation pipelines using tools like n8n,
Python, and LLM APIs to remove repetitive manual work.

## 3. RAG / knowledge retrieval systems

We build retrieval-augmented generation systems that ground AI responses
in a company's actual documentation, policies, and product data —
reducing hallucination and keeping answers accurate and up to date.

## 4. Custom AI agent development

For businesses with more specialized needs, we design custom AI agents
that can reason over multiple tools, APIs, and data sources to complete
multi-step tasks autonomously, with human approval checkpoints for
high-impact actions.

## 5. Internal dashboards and reporting automation

We build lightweight internal dashboards (ticket views, analytics,
operational reporting) so teams can monitor automated systems without
needing to read raw logs or databases.

## What's not included

We do not build systems that take irreversible high-impact actions (like
issuing refunds or making payments) without a human approval step — this
is a core design principle across everything we build, not just a
limitation.
